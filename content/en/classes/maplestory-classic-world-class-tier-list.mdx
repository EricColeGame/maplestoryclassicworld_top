## How We Ranked Every Job in This MapleStory Classic World Class Tier List

Choosing a main is the most expensive decision you will make in MapleStory Classic World. Expensive in hours, in mesos, and in scrolls you will never get back. A MapleStory Classic World class tier list is only useful if it explains *why* a job lands where it does, so every placement below is measured against the same five criteria rather than vibes or nostalgia.

The rankings here reflect **community reports and player experience** from classic-era servers, not official developer statements. Nexon publishes patch notes and event details on [the official MapleStory site](https://maplestory.nexon.net/), but it has never published a balance tier list — that work belongs to the players.

| Criterion | What It Actually Measures | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Mobbing and farming | Clear speed on training maps and mesos earned per hour | 25% |
| Single-target bossing | Damage against Zakum, Papulatus, and similar bosses | 25% |
| Party utility | Buffs, revives, and how often you get invited to runs | 20% |
| Funding efficiency | How much gear and meso the job needs before it feels good | 15% |
| Survivability and mobility | HP pool, defensive skills, and movement options | 15% |

Two caveats matter before you read the tiers. First, classic MapleStory is a party game at the top end — a "weak" job with a great buff can be more valuable than a selfish damage dealer. Second, tier lists assume comparable funding. A funded B-tier will out-damage a broke S-tier every single time.

## Early, Mid, and Late Game: Tiers Shift With Level

A single tier list hides a simple truth: your job feels completely different at level 30 than it does at level 120. In the classic advancement structure, second job arrives around level 30, third around 70, and fourth around 120. Player experience consistently shows the rankings reshuffle at each gate.

| Level Band | Jobs That Feel Strongest | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1–30 | Warrior, Magician | High base HP or ranged attacks make early maps painless |
| 30–70 | Cleric, Assassin, Hunter | Party invites, healing, and safe ranged farming |
| 70–120 | Priest, Crusader, Chief Bandit | Utility skills and AoE mobbing come online |
| 120+ | Bishop, Night Lord, Hero | Complete kits and full bossing rotations |

If you plan to play casually, weight the early and mid game more heavily. If you are chasing endgame boss runs, the late-game column is the only one that matters.

## S-Tier Jobs and Why They Dominate

### Bishop

Bishop is the closest thing classic MapleStory has to a mandatory class. Holy Symbol accelerates party leveling for everyone in range, Heal keeps runs alive, and Resurrection salvages a wipe. Community reports consistently describe Bishops as the easiest job to find a party with and the hardest to replace.

The tradeoff is solo damage. A Bishop will farm slower and kill bosses slower than a dedicated attacker, which is why many players level a Bishop as a second character funded by a damage main. In party content, though, nobody questions the slot.

### Night Lord

Night Lord sits at the top of most damage discussions. Shadow Partner and Triple Throw combine into some of the highest single-target output in the game, and the class benefits enormously from critical-based gear. If your goal is to delete a boss health bar, this is the job.

The cost is real. Throwing stars, potions, and scrolls add up fast, and the class is fragile. Player experience suggests Night Lord is the most rewarding job to fund and one of the most punishing to play under-geared.

### Hero

Hero (the Fighter line) is the classic melee bossing pick. High HP, strong one-on-one damage, and a straightforward rotation make it forgiving for players who want to stand in front of a boss and swing. It lacks the flashy utility of a Bishop, but it never depends on anyone else to function.

## A-Tier and B-Tier Jobs: Strong Picks With Real Tradeoffs

**Dark Knight** is arguably the most underrated job in the game. Hyper Body raises party HP and MP maximums, which turns dangerous boss fights into survivable ones. Its own damage is respectable, and Berserk rewards careful play. Groups that already have a Bishop still want a Dark Knight.

**Marksman and Bowmaster** split the archer identity. Marksman leans into long-range single-target pressure, while Bowmaster offers steadier, more mobile output. Both struggle to mob efficiently compared to mages, so they shine in boss content rather than training maps.

**Arch Mage (Ice/Lightning)** is the mobbing king of the tier list. Ice-based crowd control and wide AoE skills make training fast and safe. The catch is elemental resistance — some bosses simply shrug off your best skills.

**Paladin, Shadower, Buccaneer, and Corsair** all clear the bar for endgame content, but each asks for something extra. Paladin trades damage for near-unkillable defense. Shadower runs on Meso Guard and a healthy meso budget. Buccaneer rewards positioning discipline. Corsair needs gear before its summons and ranged pressure feel impactful.

## Funding, Party Roles, and Long-Term Value

Mesos change everything. A player with limited funding gets far more value from a support job than from a damage job that needs expensive gear to function. The table below reflects common community advice for budgeting your first main.

| Funding Level | Jobs That Deliver Best Value | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Bishop, Hero, Dark Knight | Strong baseline kits with cheap core skills |
| Medium | Arch Mage (I/L), Paladin, Buccaneer | Scaling improves steadily without top gear |
| High | Night Lord, Marksman, Corsair | Damage scales hard with scrolls and stars |
| Very high | Night Lord | Every meso converts into visible output |

One more consideration: party slots are limited. A run with four damage dealers and no support usually performs worse than a balanced group. That is why support jobs sit high on this MapleStory Classic World class tier list despite modest personal damage — value to the group is value, period.

## FAQ

**What is the best class in MapleStory Classic World?**
There is no single winner, but community consensus puts Bishop, Night Lord, and Hero at the top. Bishop wins on party value, Night Lord on raw damage, and Hero on reliability. Your best pick depends on whether you want to be needed, feared, or simply effective.

**Does this MapleStory Classic World class tier list apply to solo players?**
Partly. Solo players should weight mobbing and survivability more heavily and party utility less. For solo play, Arch Mage (Ice/Lightning), Hero, and Paladin tend to outperform their tier placement, while Bishop drops.

**Can I play a B-tier job and still clear endgame content?**
Yes. Every job in this tier list can clear bosses and reach high levels. B-tier simply means the job needs more funding, more practice, or a specific party composition to match an S-tier's output.

**Which class is easiest for a brand-new player?**
Warrior-line jobs are the most forgiving early because of high HP and simple rotations. Cleric is the most socially rewarding, since parties invite you before you have gear. Avoid Night Lord as a first main unless you already have a funding source.

**Final word.** Treat this tier list as a starting map, not a verdict. Classic MapleStory rewards consistency far more than it rewards picking the "correct" job — a dedicated B-tier main with good gear will always beat an abandoned S-tier alt.
