## Why the MapleStory Classic World 3rd Job Milestone Matters

Reaching level 70 in MapleStory Classic World is not a checkpoint — it is a rite of passage. The MapleStory Classic World 3rd job advancement unlocks a brand-new skill tree, dramatically stronger attacks, and access to the hunting grounds where the real grind begins. Delay it, and you will fall behind every party you join, every boss run you attempt, and every market deal you make.

In the classic ruleset, nothing is handed to you. There are no free SP resets, no instant warps to distant continents, and no catch-up mechanics that carry you to level 70 overnight. That is exactly why the third job matters so much: it is the first milestone that proves you genuinely understand the game. Community reports consistently describe level 70 as the moment a casual character becomes a real one.

The advancement also changes your identity. A Cleric becomes a Priest and suddenly becomes the most wanted player in any party. A Fighter becomes a Crusader and starts hitting numbers that make training partners jealous. Whatever class you play, the jump from second to third job is the largest power spike you will feel before the late game.

| Aspect | Before (2nd Job) | After (3rd Job) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill tree | Limited and foundational | Expanded with signature class skills |
| Damage output | Modest, mostly single-target | Much higher, frequently multi-target |
| Training zones | Victoria Island and mid-level maps | El Nath, Ludibrium, and deeper Ossyria |
| Party role | Filler damage or basic support | Defined role: healer, mobber, burst, or tank |
| Gear expectations | Clean drops and cheap scrolls | Scrolled weapons, throwing stars, stat gear |
| Market value | Low | You can finally farm items worth selling |

## MapleStory Classic World 3rd Job Requirements and Prerequisites

On paper, the checklist is short. In practice, players trip over the small details — forgetting a quest step, sailing to the wrong continent, or arriving in El Nath with no potions and no way home.

- **Level 70 minimum.** This is a hard gate. There is no workaround, no quest shortcut, and no NPC who will bend the rules for you.
- **A completed 2nd job advancement.** Your branch was chosen at level 30, and your third job is determined by that choice.
- **The quest chain started.** Talk to your 2nd job instructor on Victoria Island before you travel. Arriving in El Nath without the quest flagged is the single most common wasted trip.
- **Travel funds and supplies.** Ship tickets, return scrolls, and a healthy stack of potions all matter more than most players expect.
- **Inventory space.** Quest items and rewards need somewhere to live.
- **Patience.** The trip itself takes real time, and the ship crossing is famous for its own hazards.

| Requirement | What It Means | Common Pitfall |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 70 | Hard minimum with no exceptions | Attempting to advance at 69 |
| 2nd job complete | Branch already locked in at level 30 | Assuming you can switch paths at 70 |
| Quest chain active | Instructor dialogue must be triggered first | Sailing to El Nath with no quest |
| Correct class instructor | Each branch has its own NPC in El Nath | Speaking to the wrong instructor |
| Proof of strength | A trial item or kill objective must be completed | Vendoring or dropping the quest item |
| Travel supplies | Ship fare, return scrolls, potions | Stranded in the snow with no mesos |

## The 3rd Job Advancement Walkthrough

The order of operations matters. Follow this sequence and you will avoid the classic rookie detours.

| Step | Where | What to Do | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Any town | Restock potions, return scrolls, and clear inventory space | Prevents a wasted round trip |
| 2 | Victoria Island | Talk to your 2nd job instructor and start the chain | Flags the quest before you travel |
| 3 | Victoria Island dock | Buy a ticket and board the ship to Orbis | The only reliable route to Ossyria |
| 4 | Ship deck | Stay alert — community reports warn of mid-voyage monster attacks | Dying on the ship costs you time and EXP |
| 5 | Orbis snowfields | Follow the snowy path north into El Nath | Long walk, so bring speed gear if you have it |
| 6 | El Nath | Speak with your class's 3rd job instructor | Officially begins the advancement trial |
| 7 | El Nath region | Complete the trial and secure the required proof | The gate between you and the new skill tree |
| 8 | El Nath | Turn in the proof and confirm the advancement | Your job title changes immediately |
| 9 | Skill window | Allocate your saved SP into the new tree | SP cannot be freely reset in classic rulesets |

Player experience suggests doing the entire run in one sitting. Splitting it across sessions is how quest flags get lost, items get misplaced, and confusion sets in.

## Which 3rd Job Do You Get? Branch Map and Comparison

One thing surprises returning players: you do not choose a new branch at level 70. Your third job is the direct evolution of your second job, which means your level 30 decision echoes all the way to the endgame.

For players weighing which third job feels easiest to level, community consensus tends to rank mobbing classes and the Priest near the top, while single-target specialists feel slower solo but shine in boss content.

## Skill Builds, Gear, and Training After the MapleStory Classic World 3rd Job

### Skill Point Priorities

Your third job gives you a fresh tree and roughly three SP per level. Because classic rulesets rarely hand out free resets, every point counts.

- **Max your mastery skill first.** Weapon or magic mastery stabilizes your damage range and is the least glamorous, most valuable investment you can make.
- **Then max your primary attack.** Pick the skill you will spam for the next fifty levels and commit to it.
- **Then take buffs and utility.** Speed, defense, and party buffs make the next stretch of leveling smoother.
- **Avoid spreading points thin.** A half-finished skill usually performs worse than a maxed one.

| Skill Category | Priority | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mastery | First | Stabilizes minimum damage across every attack |
| Primary attack | Second | Directly determines your leveling speed |
| Mobility or buffs | Third | Quality-of-life that compounds over hundreds of levels |
| Situational utility | Last | Useful, but only after the core is finished |

### Gear Checklist

| Slot | What to Look For | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon | Class-appropriate attack or magic attack, scrolled | Your single biggest damage upgrade |
| Throwing stars | Higher-tier stars for thieves | Attack stars are expensive and worth it |
| Armor | Stat-boosting tops, pants, and overalls | Scroll them gradually, not all at once |
| Accessories | Earrings, capes, and rings with your main stat | Small gains that add up |
| Hat | Stat or speed bonuses | Speed helps enormously in El Nath |
| Consumables | Potions, return scrolls, ammo | Never travel to Ossyria without them |

### Where to Train

| Level Range | Recommended Spots | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 70–75 | El Nath snowfields and zombie-style maps | Dense spawns, manageable damage |
| 75–85 | Ludibrium clocktower areas | Strong mob density for multi-target classes |
| 85–95 | Ludibrium factory and watch-type maps | Consistent EXP with party synergy |
| 95–105 | Leafre and high-level Ossyria fields | Where the real endgame grind starts |

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Sailing to El Nath before starting the quest chain with your 2nd job instructor.
- Forgetting to bring return scrolls and getting stuck on the wrong continent.
- Dumping SP into a skill you will never use instead of maxing mastery.
- Ignoring party play — the MapleStory Classic World 3rd job opens doors, but parties open them faster.
- Underestimating travel time; the Orbis crossing is not a quick trip.

## FAQ

**What level do you need for the MapleStory Classic World 3rd job?**
You need to be level 70 with your 2nd job advancement already completed. There is no shortcut, and the game will not let you start the trial early.

**Can I pick a different branch at the MapleStory Classic World 3rd job?**
No. Your third job is determined entirely by the branch you selected at level 30. A Fighter always becomes a Crusader, a Cleric always becomes a Priest, and so on.

**Is the advancement trial difficult?**
It is more of a time and preparation test than a brutal skill check. Bring potions, bring return scrolls, and consider bringing a friend — the trip itself is usually the hardest part.

**What should I do right after advancing?**
Allocate your SP into mastery and your main attack skill, then head to a training zone that matches your new power level. Community reports suggest Ludibrium and the El Nath snowfields are the most popular first stops for freshly advanced characters.
