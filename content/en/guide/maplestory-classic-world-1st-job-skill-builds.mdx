One wrong SP click at level 10 can haunt you for twenty levels. In a classic-rate server, where every kill takes longer and mesos are scarce, planning your **MapleStory Classic World 1st job skill builds** before you advance is the single cheapest upgrade you can make. This guide walks through the SP order for every first job, the skills worth maxing, and the ones you should leave at zero.

Because **MapleStory Classic World 1st job skill builds** are locked in until you find an SP reset scroll, the goal here is simple: spend every point once, spend it correctly, and never look back.

## Why Your First Job Build Matters More Than You Think

The first job is short — roughly level 10 to 30 for most classes, level 8 for Magicians — but it sets the pace for everything after. You get one skill point at the job advance and three per level afterward, which means a limited pool that has to cover both utility and damage.

Two things make early mistakes expensive in a classic-style server:

- **Passive stat skills compound.** Skills like Improving Max HP Increase and Improving Max MP Increase add stats as you level up. Delaying them permanently reduces your total pool.
- **Resets are not free.** SP reset scrolls typically come from the cash shop or special events, so most players live with their choices.

Community reports from classic-server veterans consistently agree on one rule: max your passive stat-growth skill first, then your main attack. Everything else is negotiable.

## The Golden Rules of SP Order

Before diving into individual classes, internalize these five rules. They apply to every single **MapleStory Classic World 1st job skill build** on this list.

| Rule | What It Means | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Passive first | Max HP/MP growth passives before attacks | Compounds on every level-up |
| One attack, maxed | Pick one main attack and take it to max | Damage scales with skill level, not spread |
| One-point wonders | Some skills are useful at level 1 only | Saves points for damage |
| No spreading | Avoid putting 5 points into five skills | You end up weak at everything |
| Version check | Skill caps vary slightly by patch | Confirm caps in-game before committing |

The "one-point wonder" concept trips up a lot of new players. Dark Sight, Disorder, and Dash are classic examples: they do their job at a low investment, and extra points add very little.

## Warrior 1st Job Skill Build

Warriors are the most forgiving class to build, but the ordering still matters. Improving Max HP Increase should be your very first max, because the HP it grants applies as you continue leveling.

| Level Range | Skill | Points | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10–13 | Improving Max HP Increase | 10 (max) | Do this first, no exceptions |
| 13–20 | Slash Blast | 20 (max) | Hits multiple enemies — your mobbing tool |
| 20–27 | Power Strike | 20 (max) | Single-target damage for bosses and tanky mobs |
| 27–30 | Iron Body / Endure / HP Recovery | Remainder | Pick based on preference |

Some players prefer Power Strike before Slash Blast because it front-loads single-target damage. Player experience suggests the opposite for solo leveling: mobs are where the EXP is, so Slash Blast first is generally smoother. Either order works — just make sure Max HP Increase is finished early.

## Magician 1st Job Skill Build

Magicians advance at level 8, two levels earlier than everyone else, which gives them a small head start on their SP pool.

| Level Range | Skill | Points | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8–11 | Improving Max MP Increase | 10 (max) | Max MP compounds per level |
| 11–18 | Magic Claw | 20 (max) | Multi-hit, MP-efficient main attack |
| 18–25 | Magic Guard | 10–20 | Redirects damage to MP — huge survivability |
| 25–30 | MP Recovery / Magic Armor / Energy Bolt | Remainder | 1 point in Energy Bolt for utility |

Magic Claw is the clear winner over Energy Bolt for leveling. It hits multiple times, which means more consistent damage and better interaction with critical-style mechanics later. Magic Guard is the sleeper pick — a single point already changes how you survive, and maxing it makes squishy early leveling far less punishing.

## Bowman 1st Job Skill Build

Bowmen have the most debated 1st job build in classic MapleStory, mostly because of the Double Shot versus Arrow Blow argument.

| Level Range | Skill | Points | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10–13 | The Eye of Amazon | Max | Attack range — huge quality-of-life |
| 13–20 | Critical Shot | Max | Passive damage multiplier |
| 20–27 | Double Shot | 20 (max) | Fires two arrows, strong overall DPS |
| 27–30 | Arrow Blow / Focus | Remainder | 1 point in Arrow Blow early for knockback |

The Eye of Amazon is not optional. Extra attack range means you start shooting before mobs reach you, which reduces potion usage dramatically in the early game. Critical Shot applies to your main attack and scales your damage as you level.

On the Double Shot versus Arrow Blow question: community reports differ by version on whether Critical Shot interacts with Arrow Blow. The safest path is to max Double Shot and drop a single point into Arrow Blow for the knockback utility.

## Thief 1st Job Skill Build

Thieves split into two paths at the second job, and your 1st job build should match the path you intend to take.

| Path | Max First | Then | Leftovers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assassin (claw) | Lucky Seven (max) | Nimble Feet | Dark Sight, Disorder |
| Bandit (dagger) | Double Stab (max) | Nimble Feet | Disorder, Dark Sight |

Lucky Seven throws multiple stars and is the backbone of Assassin leveling. Double Stab does the same job for Bandits with a dagger. Nimble Feet gives a burst of movement speed and jump, which is genuinely useful for dodging and traveling — but it does not need to be maxed early.

Dark Sight and Disorder are classic one-point wonders. Put a point in each when convenient and move on.

## Pirate 1st Job Skill Build

If Pirates are available in your version, the class splits immediately into two weapon paths.

| Path | Max First | Then | Leftovers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brawler (knuckle) | Somersault Kick | Bullet Time | Dash |
| Gunslinger (gun) | Double Shot | Bullet Time | Dash |

Bullet Time is the passive every Pirate wants, since it improves your hit and avoid rates. Dash is another one-point-wonder style skill — a small investment for solid mobility.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Delaying the HP/MP passive | You lose permanent stat growth | Max it in your first 3–4 levels |
| Spreading SP across many skills | Nothing reaches useful power | Focus one attack to max |
| Maxing utility skills early | Damage lags behind mobs | Leave utility at 1 point until later |
| Skipping Eye of Amazon | Less range means more potions | Max it first as a Bowman |
| Ignoring version caps | You waste points past the cap | Confirm skill caps in-game |

## Where to Spend Those Levels

Your build only pays off if you train in the right place. As a rough, community-tested progression:

| Level | Suggested Area Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 10–15 | Low-HP mob fields near Henesys or Ellinia | Fast, safe kills |
| 15–20 | Mushroom-heavy maps | Dense spawns for Slash Blast and Magic Claw |
| 20–25 | Kerning swamp and boar fields | Better EXP per kill |
| 25–30 | Ant Tunnel and mid-tier mob maps | Solid EXP, good drop value |

If you are ever unsure, follow the mobs your main attack can kill in two or three hits. Efficiency beats bragging rights.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I reset my first job skills in MapleStory Classic World?**

Usually, yes — but not for free. SP reset scrolls typically come from the cash shop or limited-time events, and in classic-style servers they may be restricted or expensive. That is why planning your **MapleStory Classic World 1st job skill builds** before level 10 is far better than fixing them later.

**Which first job is easiest for a brand-new player?**

Warrior is the most forgiving. Improving Max HP Increase gives you a large health pool, Slash Blast handles mobs, and Power Strike covers single targets. Magician is also beginner-friendly if you max Magic Guard early.

**Do I need to max every skill?**

No. Most first jobs have more total skill levels than you have SP. Max your passive stat skill and your main attack, invest modestly in one or two utility skills, and leave the rest at a single point.

**Is the build different for Assassins and Bandits?**

Yes. Assassins max Lucky Seven, while Bandits max Double Stab. Both want Nimble Feet, and both treat Dark Sight and Disorder as low-investment utility. Decide your second job path before you spend your first point at level 10.
